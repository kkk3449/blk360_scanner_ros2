# Cover Letter — Revised Manuscript (sensors-4464625), submitted 16 Aug 2026

Dear Editor,

We are pleased to resubmit our revised manuscript, now entitled "Occlusion-Aware Visibility Coverage for Robotic Stop-and-Scan 3D LiDAR Mapping" (sensors-4464625), for the Special Issue "Recent Progress in 3D Computer Vision and Robotics" (Guest Editor: Prof. Dr. Antonio Adan) of Sensors. We thank the editor and the three reviewers for their careful and constructive reviews, which have substantially improved the manuscript.

The major changes are: (1) the title has been revised as suggested by Reviewer 1, and the contribution is now explicitly scoped as 2D scan-station planning for subsequent 3D mapping (new Section 3.5); (2) a new experiment was added — a disk marginal-gain baseline governed by the identical accept/skip rule as the proposed policy, which isolates the coverage model itself as the source of the placement difference (Sections 4.2 and 6.2); (3) an explicit research question with subquestions and a structure roadmap now close the Introduction; (4) the equations, figures, references, and English have been revised throughout as requested. A point-by-point response to each reviewer accompanies this resubmission, and all changes are visible in the marked-up manuscript.

Since the original submission, the preliminary conference version has been accepted for presentation at the 2026 International Conference on Control, Automation and Systems (ICCAS; Regular Paper, decision of 31 July 2026). Its status has been updated in the manuscript, and the accepted conference manuscript is provided for the editors and reviewers. The overlap is disclosed in the Introduction; the visibility coverage model, the controlled ablation, the parameter analysis, and all hardware and registration experiments are new to this submission.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by any other journal. All authors have approved the revised manuscript. The authors' relationships with CASELAB Co., Ltd. are fully disclosed in the Conflicts of Interest statement.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
